My Engineering Approach

When working on engineering projects, I try to understand the problem, develop a solution, and carefully test the results.

  1. Understand the problem and its requirements.
  2. Research possible solutions and choose an approach.
  3. Implement the design or program.
  4. Test and debug the results.
  5. Document what worked and what could improve.
